# Business Model

Customers

* Recreational tennis players
* League players
* Tournament players
* Tennis clubs
* Coaches
* Tournament organizers

Value Proposition

Players save time by managing their entire tennis life from one place.

Instead of checking multiple websites, calendars, and emails, Semut Tennis provides one assistant that understands player preferences.

Revenue

Free

* League discovery
* Personal dashboard
* Match history
* Calendar synchronization

Premium

* AI scheduling assistant
* Registration reminders
* Cross-platform statistics
* Match recommendations
* Travel planning
* Personalized analytics

Future Opportunities

* Club software integrations
* Coach tools
* Tournament management
* Referral partnerships
* Premium APIs

Long-term Strategy

The business is not limited to tennis.

Semut Tennis validates the broader Semut concept:

One AI assistant coordinating multiple independent organizations on behalf of the user.
