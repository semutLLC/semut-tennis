# Semut Tennis Vision

Vision

Semut Tennis is an AI coordination assistant that helps recreational tennis players navigate fragmented tennis ecosystems.

Rather than replacing existing organizations, Semut Tennis connects them.

Players often interact with multiple independent systems:

* USTA leagues
* Local clubs
* Recreation departments
* Tournament organizers
* Court reservation systems
* Calendar applications
* Email notifications

Each system contains part of the player’s history.

Semut Tennis creates a unified personal tennis workspace that remembers player preferences, coordinates schedules, and helps players discover and participate in more tennis.

Long-term Vision

A player should be able to simply say:

“I’m a 3.5 player. I can play Tuesday evenings and Saturday mornings within 30 minutes of home.”

Semut Tennis should then:

* discover suitable leagues
* notify registration deadlines
* avoid schedule conflicts
* coordinate tournaments
* maintain one calendar
* recommend opportunities
* keep a complete playing history

The player interacts with one assistant instead of many disconnected websites.

Research Goal

Semut Tennis serves as the first implementation of Semut’s broader coordination architecture.

The lessons learned from coordinating tennis organizations can later be applied to other fragmented domains such as healthcare, education, local businesses, volunteering, and government services.
